MULTI-SIGNAL ENGINE

More than a blacklist.

PhishLens evaluates different characteristics of a destination and combines them into an explainable security decision.

01

URL Structure

Detect insecure protocols, IP-based destinations, suspicious paths, encoded characters and phishing language.

02

Domain DNA

Inspect domain identity, trust status, subdomains and lookalike patterns.

03

Redirect Intelligence

Follow server-side redirects and expose the final destination before the user clicks.

04

UPI Shield

Inspect UPI payment information for suspicious verification and account language.

05

QR Intelligence

Decode QR destinations and pass the resulting URL through the same security engine.

06

Explainable AI

Turn technical indicators into a simple explanation that users can understand.
