        HOW PHISHLENS THINKS
    
    


    
    

        From destination
        
            to decision.
        

    
    


    
    

        PhishLens breaks a destination into multiple
        independent security signals instead of relying
        on one simple blacklist check.

    
    


    
    


        

        
    

            
    
                01 / INSPECT
            
    


            
    
                ↗
            
    


            
    
                Inspect
            
    


            
    

                Examine protocol, URL structure,
                parameters, subdomains and suspicious
                patterns.

            
    

        
    



        

        
    

            
    
                02 / ANALYZE
            
    


            
    
                ◇
            
    


            
    
                Analyze
            
    


            
    

                Combine independent signals into
                a transparent risk score.

            
    

        
    



        

        
    

            
    
                03 / EXPLAIN
            
    


            
    
                ⓘ
            
    


            
    
                Explain
            
    


            
    

                Convert technical indicators into
                a clear human-readable explanation.

            
    

        
    



        

        
    

            
    
                04 / PROTECT
            
    


            
    
                ◉
            
    


            
    
                Protect
            
    


            
    

                Help users recognize risky destinations
                before sharing credentials or payment
                information.

            
    

        
    

    
    


    






    

    
    
        MULTI-SIGNAL ENGINE
    
    


    
    

        More than a
        blacklist.

    
    


    
    

        PhishLens evaluates different characteristics
        of a destination and combines them into an
        explainable security decision.

    
    


    
    


        
    

            
                01
            

            
    
                URL Structure
            
    

            
    
                Detect insecure protocols, IP-based
                destinations, suspicious paths,
                encoded characters and phishing language.
            
    

        
    


        
    

            
                02
            

            
    
                Domain DNA
            
    

            
    
                Inspect domain identity, trust status,
                subdomains and lookalike patterns.
            
    

        
    


        
    

            
                03
            

            
    
                Redirect Intelligence
            
    

            
    
                Follow server-side redirects and expose
                the final destination before the user clicks.
            
    

        
    


        
    

            
                04
            

            
    
                UPI Shield
            
    

            
    
                Inspect UPI payment information for
                suspicious verification and account language.
            
    

        
    


        
    

            
                05
            

            
    
                QR Intelligence
            
    

            
    
                Decode QR destinations and pass the
                resulting URL through the same security engine.
            
    

        
    


        
    

            
                06
            

            
    
                Explainable Results
            
    

            
    
                Show the checks and reasons returned by the
                analysis in language people can understand.
